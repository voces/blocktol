Privacy & the data we collect

Last updated 13 July 2026

Blocktol is a daily maze-building game with no accounts — no sign-up, no email, no password. This page explains exactly what we store, why, and how you can get a copy of it or delete it.

Who you are to us

When you first open the game your device generates a random anonymous ID and keeps it in your browser's local storage. That ID is how we recognise your builds and stats across visits. We don't know your name (unless you type a display name), your email, or your identity — just this random ID.

What we store

  • Your anonymous ID — the random identifier above.
  • Display name — a random one to start; you can change it.
  • Your gameplay — the mazes you build, their times, and which attempts counted, so we can score the daily and show leaderboards.
  • Preferences — theme, zoom, and notification settings.
  • Rating — your ELO-style score across days played.
  • Language/region — your browser locale, so notifications read in your language. Your timezone is used while you play to pick the right daily, but is not stored.
  • Push subscriptions — only if you turn on notifications: the browser push endpoint needed to deliver them.
  • Diagnostics — to keep the game fast and reliable, our servers record performance traces and error logs. These may include a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of your anonymous ID — never your name or the ID itself — so we can follow a single session when debugging. They live on our own infrastructure and are automatically deleted after about 30 days.

Cookies

We don't use cookies, and we don't run any advertising or third-party tracking. The only thing stored on your device is the functional data the game needs to work (your ID, your in-progress build, and your settings), so there is no cookie banner to click through.

Who else sees it

Our own servers and database run the game. Beyond that, a small number of services may process limited data on our behalf:

  • Error monitoring — when something crashes, a diagnostic report (including your anonymous ID) is sent to our error-monitoring provider so we can fix it.
  • Push delivery — if you enable notifications, your browser's push service (e.g. Google, Mozilla, or Apple) delivers them.
  • Fonts — the game's typeface is loaded from Google Fonts.

We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can do both of these yourself, any time, from Profile → Account:

Export my data

Downloads a JSON file with everything we hold about you — your profile, every run, your notifications, and your push subscriptions.

Delete my data

Permanently removes your profile, name, preferences, and push subscriptions, and unlinks every maze you've built from you. Your builds stay on past leaderboards as an anonymous player — with no way to connect them back to you — so the historical results of days other people competed in aren't rewritten. This can't be undone, and your device starts over as a brand-new player.

How long we keep it

Your profile and builds are kept while you keep playing, so your stats and history stay intact. Delete your data (above) to remove them sooner. Diagnostic traces and logs are short-lived — automatically deleted after about 30 days.

Contact
